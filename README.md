# Geneva Ecola — personal website

Open `index.html` to view the site, or serve this folder with a static web server.
To update GitHub Pages, copy this folder's contents into the website repository.
The existing `CNAME` is included.

Entries put the school, lab, company, or organization and title in one heading,
with a logo on the left and dates on the right. Stanford Ph.D. and M.S. degrees
share one entry, with both dates retained. Text is larger throughout the entries.
Service photos sit on the right, vertically centered alongside their full lists
on desktop, and stack below the lists on narrower screens.

The uploaded photos are included locally:

- `images/mobisys-2026-group.png` beside Professional Service (ACM MobiSys 2026 group photo).
- `images/stanford-splash.jpg` beside University Service.

School and company logos use local files only. Add your seven logo files to
`images/logos/`; see `images/logos/README.md` for the expected filenames.
Text labels appear until those files are supplied. There are no online logo
sources, downloader scripts, or required build steps.

The site retains the original CV, publication figures, contact links, analytics,
section navigation, colors, and responsive layout.

The MobiSys group photo uses a shorter 4:3 display crop that removes excess
space above the group. Clicking it opens the full original image.

Research retains the doctoral researcher/lab entry without Research Interests
cards. About Me retains its original text. Each publication has a short takeaway; the thermal-noise paper
also links to the Stanford Report coverage.
